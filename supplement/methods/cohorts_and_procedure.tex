Table~\ref{tab:cohorts} separates the populations. D used 40 training, 16 validation, eight calibration, 32 test, and 16 out-of-distribution episodes. Four calibration episodes set the peak margin and four set probability calibration. Training data, residual scale, checkpoint choice, and correction strength excluded the test set. The supplementary protocol record maps the cohort labels to their source configurations; these cohorts are not training-seed replications of one experiment.

Table~\ref{tab:procedure} gives the diagnostic inputs, branches, and outputs. A request identifier connects a candidate choice to its consumed timing row and application outcome. Episode pairing then connects those outcomes to cost and temperature changes. Deadline expiration and score-order changes require different follow-up evidence; neither can be inferred from a pooled timing quantile alone.
